# Reports and summaries

## Purpose

Household members see the total spent in a month, in LKR.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F5.1 As a household member, I see the total spent in a month, in LKR.
- F5.2 As a household member, I pick the month to see, and the current month is shown first.
- F5.3 As a household member, I see the monthly total as a table and a simple chart.
- F5.4 As a household member, I see the month's spending chart as a bar per day.
- F5.5 As a household member, I see the previous month's total beside the chosen month's.

## Decisions

- Amounts are converted to LKR as P4 requires.
- Reports cannot be exported.
- A month with no expenses shows a total of zero.

## Out of Scope

- Breakdowns by category or by person.
- A month-by-month trend over several months.
- Top merchants.
- Exporting a report.
- Custom date ranges.
