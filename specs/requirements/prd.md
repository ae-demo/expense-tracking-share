# Household Expense Tracker

## Problem Statement

Two partners who share a household have no single place to see what they spend. Expenses sit in separate receipts, memories and apps, so neither can see the whole picture or how much is going where.

## Solution

A small web app for two people to record household expenses together, including by uploading a photo of a bill, and see shared spending, reports and budgets in one place.

## Actors

- Household member: one of the two partners. Signs in, and can see, add, edit and delete every expense, category and budget. Both have equal access.
